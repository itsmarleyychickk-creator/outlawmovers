OUTLAW MOVERS LLC - WINDOWS OFFICE APP

1. Install Node.js 24 LTS or newer from https://nodejs.org.
   Choose the Windows installer and keep its default settings.

2. Extract ALL files from the ZIP into a folder you control, such as
   Documents\Outlaw-Movers-Office. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
   If an office folder already exists, do not replace its data folder.

3. Double-click START-OFFICE.bat.
   The office login opens in your browser. Your generated local office
   password opens in Notepad. Copy it into the login form.
   Keep the command window open while working. Close Notepad after login.

4. Start with Business setup, then work through the company, carrier,
   insurance, fleet and driver records. Imported PDF fields are unverified.
   Follow official links to submit actual agency filings.

5. Use Employees, Equipment, Customers, Transport jobs, Finance and
   Departments for everyday work. Records save on this computer.

BACKUPS
Opening the launcher makes a daily database backup when a database exists.
The backups folder is on the same computer. Regularly copy the whole office
folder to a secure external drive or another protected location. If the PC
fails, backups on the same PC may be lost. Keep this package private:
it contains business identifiers, source paperwork and saved records.

IMPORTANT
- This is one-computer operation. Do not upload its data, backups or PDF
  to GitHub or put the office folder on a public website.
- There is one shared office password. Staff access roles are not implemented.
- The public website cannot send inquiries to this local office automatically.
  Customers email you; log received inquiries in the local office app.
- Reminders appear when you open the office; calendar export helps you keep
  deadlines in a calendar. The app does not send reminder emails.
- No payroll processing, bank connection, tax submission or online payment
  processing is included.
- To open again, double-click START-OFFICE.bat. Do not re-extract over your data.

If Windows shows an error, share the error text, not passwords or private IDs.
