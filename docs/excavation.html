BUILT ON THE RIGHT GROUNDWORK

The right depth for your project.

Groundwork done to the required depth. We always dig trenches, footers and runners to the depth required for your project, following applicable code, site conditions, approved plans and manufacturer’s installation instructions. We confirm the project requirements before digging.

For conventional exterior residential footings in McCracken, Christian and Trigg counties, Kentucky’s standard minimum frost-protection depth is 24 inches, measured from proposed finished grade to the bottom of the footing. Some Kentucky counties require 27–33 inches. Approved alternative foundation designs and code exceptions may apply.

Manufactured-home footers and runners follow the home’s installation instructions and approved foundation design, including required frost protection. Footing thickness, width and reinforcement are separate design requirements. Utility trenches follow the specific utility’s requirements; Kentucky water services require at least 30 inches of installation depth. Bedding and pipe dimensions can require a deeper excavation. Plumbing and electrical connections are handled by the appropriate providers or trades.

References: Kentucky Residential Code R403.1.4 and Table R403.1.4; 815 KAR 25:090; 815 KAR 20:120, Section 12. Requirements checked October 15, 2026; the applicable plans and reviewing authority determine the requirements for each job.
