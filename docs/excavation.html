HOW WE GET IT DONE

Good groundwork starts with a clear plan.

01 · THE OUTLAW WAY

Tell us what needs digging.

Share the job location, purpose, approximate size and depth if known. Photos or plans help us understand the work.

02 · THE OUTLAW WAY

Look at the site together.

We’ll discuss access, soil, nearby structures, drainage and where excavated material will go. We’ll confirm the details needed for a quote.

03 · THE OUTLAW WAY

Get ready before the bucket moves.

We’ll agree on the scope, price, timing and responsibilities for utility locating and any required approvals. Digging begins after the agreed preparations are complete.

04 · THE OUTLAW WAY

Prepare the way for the next crew.

We complete the agreed excavation and review the work with you. Any backfilling, material removal or finish grading is confirmed in your quote.

Our work is excavation and ground preparation. Plumbing, electrical connections and septic installation are handled by the appropriate providers or trades.
