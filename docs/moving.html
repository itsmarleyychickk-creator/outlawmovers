HOW YOUR MOVE COMES TOGETHER

From the first call to the new location.

01 · TELL US ABOUT IT

Start with your move.

Share what you need moved, the pickup and destination locations, approximate dimensions and your preferred timing. Photos help us get a first look.

02 · WORK THROUGH THE DETAILS

Make a practical plan.

We’ll discuss the structure, access at both properties, the route and any site preparation. We’ll let you know if a site visit is needed to assess the move.

03 · KNOW WHAT TO EXPECT

Review your quote.

We’ll work through the proposed scope, price, schedule and responsibilities with you, including preparation and required approvals, before confirming the work.

04 · GET READY

Prepare for moving day.

We’ll confirm the agreed preparation and access arrangements. Utility disconnections and reconnections must be arranged with the appropriate providers or trades before and after the move.

05 · MAKE YOUR NEXT MOVE

Bring the plan together.

Once the agreed preparations and required approvals are in place, we coordinate the scheduled move and delivery arrangements with you. Placement and any setup work are confirmed in your written scope.

Every structure and property is different. Contact us to discuss whether your move is suitable, what’s included and how we can help with site preparation. We do not perform plumbing or electrical connections.
